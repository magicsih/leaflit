# A quieter place to read

Leaflit opens local Markdown files with room for the words.

## Reading essentials

Use **Find** to move through a document. Select a sentence to copy it, or adjust the text size from the View menu. 한글과 emoji 🌿 are welcome here.

- [x] Read a local document
- [x] Keep your source unchanged
- [ ] Explore the [next page](Next%20Page.markdown#the-next-step)

> Your file stays on your Mac. Diagrams and formulas work offline.
